High Retention

Leitner Box Method

Flashcard study system that helps you focus more on the information you struggle with. You place flashcards into different boxes based on how well you know them. Cards you know well are reviewed less often, while harder cards are reviewed more frequently until you remember them.

Most Effective

Active Recall

You test yourself instead of just rereading notes. You try to remember information from memory by answering questions, explaining concepts, or using flashcards. This strengthens your ability to recall information during exams.

Most Popular

Pomodoro Technique

Time-management method that breaks studying into short focused sessions. Typically, you study for 25 minutes and then take a 5-minute break. After completing several sessions, you take a longer break to rest and recharge.

Deep Learning

Feynman Techniques

Helps you understand difficult topics by explaining them in simple words. You pretend you are teaching the concept to someone else and identify areas where your explanation is unclear. This helps you find gaps in your knowledge and improve your understanding.
